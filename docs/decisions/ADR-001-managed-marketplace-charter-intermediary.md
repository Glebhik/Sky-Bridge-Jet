# ADR-001: Managed marketplace and charter intermediary

## Status

Accepted — Product Owner Approved

## Context

V1 connects customers, delegates, and concierge users with private aviation
operators and brokers but does not own or operate aircraft.

## Decision

Sky Bridge Jet will operate as a managed private-aviation marketplace and
charter intermediary. The platform owns digital orchestration; licensed
operators remain responsible for aircraft operation and flight execution.

## Consequences

Core workflows support requests, offers, selection, booking and payment
orchestration, notifications, concierge services, and Empty Legs. Provider
ports preserve future direct integrations and deeper automation without
rewriting the domain.

## Deferred / Requires Specialist Review

Specialist aviation and commercial counsel must determine the exact legal,
agency, intermediary, contractual, and regulatory characterization before
production launch. This ADR makes no such legal claim.
