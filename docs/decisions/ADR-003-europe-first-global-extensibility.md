# ADR-003: Europe-first with global extensibility

## Status

Accepted — Product Owner Approved

## Context

V1 commercial focus is Europe, with future global expansion expected.

## Decision

Prioritize Ireland, the United Kingdom, France, Monaco-area airports,
Switzerland, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, and
Belgium. Model country, currency, timezone, airport, operator, and regulatory
concepts without Europe-only assumptions.

## Consequences

V1 product prioritization is Europe-first while data and provider boundaries
remain suitable for later regional expansion.

## Deferred / Requires Specialist Review

Launch-country obligations, aviation rules, privacy, tax, sanctions, and
payment requirements require qualified regional review.
